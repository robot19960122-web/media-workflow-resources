## Procedure

1. Keep the original file unchanged and name each candidate distinctly.
2. Compare the same interval, including audio before and after the edit.
3. Use the same device at similar comfortable perceived volume.
4. Listen without reading the expected words; privately transcribe what you hear.
5. Check consonants, held notes and transitions separately.
6. Replay the verse and re-open the export before deciding.
7. Record the reason for the decision; do not infer rights or consent from audio quality.
