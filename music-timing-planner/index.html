How to interpret the result

In 4/4 at 120 BPM, a four-bar phrase lasts eight seconds. A 30-second gap is 3.75 phrases. Try edits at complete phrases, then listen to the join and ending. A short fade or a different cue may work better than an exact numerical match.

Phrase duration = 60 ÷ BPM × beats per bar × bars per phrase. Source and target are whole-cue durations; the calculation does not account for overlap introduced by crossfades or tempo changes.

Continuation is a different calculation

If you continue at 45 seconds and create 30 new seconds, the planned endpoint is 75 seconds. Source audio after the continuation point is replaced; it is not automatically kept and extended. Any actual transition overlap can affect the delivered duration.
