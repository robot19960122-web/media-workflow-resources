# Music removal: compare what remains

Prepared for RemoveBackgroundMusic with AI assistance. This checklist is a review aid, not an independent evaluation or a guarantee of separation quality.

## Choose the target before listening

Write down whether you need dialogue plus scene effects, or speech alone. Those are different targets. A quieter soundtrack can still be a poor result if it also removes the impact, room tone or syllable you needed. Retain the unmodified source and note the selected processing mode.

## Select difficult moments

Review a moment where music and speech overlap, a soft sentence ending, and an important transient. Compare the same time range in the original and output. Listen at a comfortable level and avoid judging only by which version is louder. Do not claim level-matched testing unless you actually performed it.

After a promising excerpt, review the complete export. A short preview does not establish quality throughout a long recording. Music reduction also does not grant a copyright license or establish that the output is safe to republish.
