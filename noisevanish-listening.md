# Speech cleanup: compare noise and voice together

Prepared for NoiseVanish with AI assistance. This is a listening worksheet, not a controlled benchmark or a promise of studio-quality sound.

## Define the problem

Identify the unwanted sound before choosing processing: steady fan or hiss, changing street noise, reverberation, clipping and a second speaker are not interchangeable problems. Write down what matters most in the delivery: intelligibility, a natural voice, consistent chapter levels or usable video synchronization.

Retain the original recording. Select a difficult passage that includes both speech and a pause. Include every speaker rather than testing only the loudest or clearest voice. Do not infer recovery of missing words from a lower noise floor.

## Compare settings without losing the reference

Begin with a lighter cleanup setting. Compare original and processed versions at the same position, then check whether stronger processing adds enough benefit to justify voice-texture changes. Avoid treating a louder result as proof of better noise reduction. A loudness target does not guarantee that every source reaches that target without constraints.
